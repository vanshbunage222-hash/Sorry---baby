01 · My Apology

I'm genuinely sorry, Baby.

I know I got angry and raised my voice. You didn't deserve that from me and I don't want to make excuses for it.

Sometimes emotions get the better of me, but that doesn't make it okay. When I care about someone this deeply, I should be able to stop for a moment, listen properly and speak with patience.

I'm sorry for the moment when I made you feel hurt instead of making you feel loved. I'm sorry if my words made your heart heavy.

The person who wants to be your comfort shouldn't become the reason you need comfort. And I understand that.

I don't expect one website or one "sorry" to magically erase everything. I just want you to know that I understand where I went wrong.

You matter to me. Your feelings matter to me. And what we have matters to me.

I'm really sorry, Baby. ❤️
02 · What You Mean To Me

You're more than just someone I love.

You have become such a beautiful part of my everyday life. Your messages, your smile, your random conversations, your little reactions, even the smallest things you do have a special place in my heart.

You are someone I don't want to hurt. You're someone I want to understand better every day.

I don't want our relationship to be about who wins an argument. I want it to be about two people who learn each other, understand each other and choose each other even after difficult moments.

You deserve to feel heard. You deserve respect. You deserve patience. And you deserve someone who can admit when he is wrong.

So today I'm not trying to prove anything. I'm simply saying:

I was wrong. I'm sorry. ❤️

04 · If I Could Be There

If I could talk to you right now...

I wouldn't start with a hundred explanations.

I'd simply tell you that I'm sorry and I'd give you the space to tell me everything you feel.

I'd listen properly. No interrupting. No defending myself. No trying to prove that I was right.

Because sometimes loving someone isn't about having the perfect words. Sometimes it's simply about caring enough to understand what the other person is feeling.

And that's something I want to keep learning.

You deserve softness from me. ❤️
05 · My Promises

Things I want to do better.

I'll listen first. Your feelings don't need to compete with my emotions.
I'll control my reactions. Being upset is normal. Hurting you with anger isn't something I want to repeat.
I'll respect your boundaries. Your comfort and feelings will always matter to me.
I'll communicate better. I'd rather have an honest conversation than let assumptions grow.
I'll appreciate you. I never want familiarity to make me forget how special you are.
I'll keep learning you. Because loving someone also means understanding them better.

I don't want a perfect relationship.

I want a real one.

One where we can laugh, have difficult days, misunderstand each other sometimes, talk things through, make memories, and still choose kindness.

I'm not asking you to forget how you felt.

I'm asking you to see that I understand where I went wrong and that I genuinely want to do better.

So if these words can reach you through this little screen, let them say the simplest thing possible:
